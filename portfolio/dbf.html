Dragon Bravo Fire

Following a recent trip to the North Rim of Grand Canyon National Park, I learned that the area, along with the Kaibab National Forest was plagued by the Dragon Bravo Fire in the summer of 2025. The fire first began on July 4th, 2025 as a result of a lightning strike near a rock formation known as "The Dragon". The fire lasted for nearly three months before full containment on September 29, 2025, burning over 145,000 acres and making it the largest wildfire in the US during 2025 (outside of Alaska).
